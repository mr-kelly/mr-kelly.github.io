Why a CAIO

Tools are abundant. The missing role connects direction, execution, and acceptance.

Leadership knows AI matters and employees are experimenting, but nobody continuously chooses the work, aligns owners, and proves the result. A fractional CAIO installs that operating mechanism.

01

Scattered direction

Departments run disconnected tool experiments.

02

Broken execution

Advice has no owner, deadline, or weekly follow-through.

03

Unproven outcomes

Demos lack baselines, tests, and business acceptance.

04

Lost assets

Knowledge stays in people and chat histories.

First 30 days

Establish a baseline before scaling

The first month does not promise a company-wide transformation. Prove one bounded scenario and use its evidence to decide what comes next.

Week 1

See the system

Map goals, process, systems, data, roles, and risk.

Week 2

Choose the pilot

Freeze one valuable, data-accessible, human-verifiable scenario.

Week 3

Close the loop

Run task definition, execution, testing, evidence, and human sign-off.

Week 4

Make the decision

Review baseline, outcome, risk, and a Go / No-Go recommendation.

Delivery and acceptance

Visible every week, decision-ready every month

Metrics are not copied from examples. Establish the current baseline, then freeze definitions, data scope, thresholds, and sign-off owners together.

Core deliverables

  • Weekly work with owners, deadlines, and definition of done
  • A real business outcome that continues to advance
  • Problem, cause, change, test, evidence, and sign-off records
  • Methods, documentation, data, Agent Skills, and next-stage roadmap

Typical evaluation dimensions

  • Delivery cycle and wait time
  • Task and evidence traceability
  • Quality, rework, and regression defects
  • Human intervention and team independence
  • Business outcomes and executive decision speed

Engagement boundaries

Accountable for progress, without unverifiable promises

01

Not a headcount-reduction program

Make work and capability visible; the client makes organization decisions from evidence.

02

No fixed efficiency multiple

Scope, data, systems, and teams differ; measure a baseline before setting targets.

03

No bypassing human release authority

AI output passes agreed tests, access controls, and human approval.

04

FDE is scoped separately

Agents, code changes, automated tests, integrations, or private deployment require a separate SOW, milestones, evals, and quote.
