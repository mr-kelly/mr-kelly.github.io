Delivery method

Define one measurable outcome, then expand

Custom engagements start with the business objective, accountable owner, data, and acceptance criteria rather than a speculative feature list.

01

Define

Clarify the problem, target users, and desired outcome.

02

Scope

Confirm training, Agents, workflows, data, and integrations.

03

Deliver

Run enablement, configuration, builds, or data work.

04

Validate

Test with a real but controlled business scenario.

05

Improve

Expand workflows and service entry points based on use.
